About Aarohan

Education that connects knowledge with action.

Aarohan College of Commerce & Management is a fictional institution created for this Digital Marketing practical assignment. The college concept focuses on academic foundations, communication, analytical thinking, digital literacy and responsible leadership.

Our learning approach combines classroom teaching with presentations, case discussions, projects, internships, student-led activities and exposure to current business practices. We aim to help students become curious learners and confident contributors.
